# 3. Password Key Protection

Date: 2026-06-13

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Some users may feel the desire to protect sensitive information or events behind a password for added security. Users may have sensitive information in their planner and wish to have an extra layer of security. 

## Decision

Enabling a password protection software. When accessing the app, a password encryption will pop up requesting the user writes in the correct password. If written incorrectly an error screen will appear, signaling no access to the application after an incorrect input. 

## Consequences

This feature will make some users feel more secure in their information being stored and block access from unwanted viewing of their information. This may provide difficulties among some users if they forget their password, forcing them to follow some form of account recovery process.
